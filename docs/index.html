Hy2droid

An Android app that connects your phone to a Hysteria 2 server — one you run, or one whose share link you were given. Paste the link or scan a QR code, and the phone's traffic goes out through that server. Needs Android 11 or newer; no root and no Google account.

AmneziaWG Web UI

A web page for running your own AmneziaWG VPN server, shipped as one ~35 MB Docker image: create servers and clients, export .conf, QR or vpn:// links, and watch traffic. Needs a Linux host with Docker, Compose 2.24 or newer and a public IPv4 address.

 Self-hosted

iMail

A mail server for your own domains: SMTP, IMAPS and POP3S, a browser inbox, an admin console and per-domain certificates. Needs Docker, a domain you control, and port 25 open at your provider.
